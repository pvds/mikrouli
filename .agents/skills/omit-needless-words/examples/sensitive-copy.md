# Example: sensitive or therapeutic copy

## Original

We understand that this may be a difficult time for you, and we want you to know that you are very
welcome to contact us whenever you feel ready to talk about what has been going on.

## Light

This may be a difficult time. You are welcome to contact us when you feel ready to talk.

## Balanced

Contact us when you feel ready to talk.

## Strong

Contact us when ready.

## Notes

- Use light intensity by default for emotionally sensitive copy unless the user explicitly asks for
  stronger compression.
- When defaulting, report the reason clearly: "Defaulted to light for emotionally sensitive copy".
- Strong intensity may be too cold for therapeutic or relationship-focused writing.
- Do not remove warmth when it helps the reader feel safe enough to act.
