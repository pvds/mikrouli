# Copy

This is the detailed writing guide. For the summary of the brand, see `docs/brand.md`; for the generated voice model, see `docs/voice-profile.md`.

## Core voice

- Calm, supportive, and practical.
- Warm without being sentimental.
- Clear without sounding clinical or overly polished.
- Human, collaborative, and culturally aware.

## Writing principles

### Therapy/public copy

- Prefer direct, reassuring language.
- Use `I` when speaking as the therapist and `you` when addressing the client.
- Lead with clarity, not jargon.
- Anchor wording in patterns, transitions, relationships, and practical next steps.
- Keep paragraphs short and easy to scan.

### Dev/docs copy

- Prefer procedural structure: clear headings, lists, quick steps.
- Compress to commands, constraints, and outcomes first.
- Explain trade-offs where they matter.
- Avoid generic hype or abstract promises.

## Preferred moves

- "Small shifts" and "meaningful change"
- "Together" and "let's explore"
- Concrete nouns: patterns, relationships, transitions, sessions, support
- Gentle reframes that explain the next step

## Banned moves

- Hype hooks and performative excitement
- Bait questions or broad generic claims
- "Not X, just Y" framing
- Buzzword-heavy language without explanation
- Urgency or FOMO language in CTAs

## CTA rules

- Keep CTAs low-pressure and clear.
- Prefer invitation, choice, and clarity.
- Good examples: "Book a free 15-minute intake", "Let's talk about what support you need."

## Structure and rhythm

- Short-to-medium sentences in therapy copy
- Structured lists and subheads for readability
- Deliberate pacing over intensity
- Prefer plain English to clinical jargon unless the term is necessary and explained

## Examples

### Good

- "I help you untangle patterns that may be keeping you stuck."
- "Let's look at what is happening between you and your partner, and what support might help most."
- "You do not need to carry this alone."

### Avoid

- "Unlock your full potential with transformative therapy."
- "Break free from your limiting patterns today."
- "The ultimate solution for modern relationship healing."
