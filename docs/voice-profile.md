VOICE PROFILE
=============
Author: Mikrouli
Goal: Keep a calm, consistent voice across therapy-facing copy, blog content, and repo docs without defaulting to generic AI tone.
Confidence: High for therapy/public copy; medium for technical/docs copy.

Source Set
- `src/data/generated/pages.json`
- `src/data/generated/posts.json`
- `src/data/generated/services.json`
- `docs/brand.md`
- `README.md`
- `docs/workflow.md`
- `docs/jsdoc.md`

Rhythm
- Short-to-medium sentences, calm pacing, and frequent section headers in therapy copy.
- Structured, procedural rhythm in dev/docs copy with bullets, tables, and clear steps.

Compression
- Medium compression overall.
- Therapy lane explains and reassures.
- Dev/docs lane compresses into commands, bullets, and rules.

Capitalization
- Conventional. Emphasis through bold and clear hierarchy, not gimmick casing.

Parentheticals
- Rare. Used only for brief qualification or a small clarifier, not for performance.

Question Use
- Low frequency. Used for reflection or gentle framing, not bait.

Claim Style
- Supportive, practical, and low-hype.
- Mechanisms like patterns, systems, and small shifts beat grand promises.
- Dev/docs claims are anchored in concrete tooling and workflow details.

Preferred Moves
- Direct second-person (`you`) and first-person therapist (`I`) in therapy copy.
- "Small shifts / meaningful change" motif.
- Clear subheads, bullets, and scoped sections.
- Empathy paired with a practical next step.
- Concrete nouns over abstract buzzwords.

Banned Moves
- Hype hooks and performative excitement
- Bait questions
- "Not X, just Y" framing
- Forced lowercase style play
- Generic founder/LinkedIn cadence
- Overly clinical jargon without explanation

CTA Rules
- Soft, low-pressure asks.
- Offer invitation, choice, and clarity.
- Avoid urgency/FOMO language.

Channel Notes
- X: Shorter; one insight and one reframe; no bait.
- LinkedIn: Calm authority; structured blocks; no motivational fluff.
- Email: Personal, warm, direct; state purpose early; close with one gentle ask.
