# Mikrouli Systemic Therapy — Brand Guide

Unified visual, tonal, and identity guidelines.

---

## 1. Brand Identity

**Brand Name**: Mikrouli Systemic Therapy

**Tagline**: "New Perspectives, Meaningful Change"

**Who I Am**

I provide calm, supportive therapy for individuals, couples, and families—especially those navigating cultural transitions. My approach blends reflection with practical steps to foster growth, connection, and self-awareness.

### Mission

I create an empathic, culturally attuned therapy space where clients explore the patterns that shape their lives. Through collaboration and reflection, I help them gain fresh perspectives and make meaningful changes.

### Vision

Support clients' emotional well-being and self-discovery through inclusive, empathetic, growth-focused practice.

- **Inclusivity & Accessibility**: Welcome clients from all backgrounds; specialized support for internationals and expats.
- **Empowerment Through Collaboration**: View individuals within broader systems; provide tools for sustainable well-being.
- **Human-Centered Care**: Value authenticity, warmth, and understanding—invite each client to be their full self.

### Core Values

1. **Human & Personal**: Honor each client's unique story with genuine warmth.
2. **Empathic & Collaborative**: Partner with clients to uncover insights; foster shared decision-making.
3. **Inclusive & Culturally Aware**: Adapt approach to each client's cultural context.
4. **Curiosity & Practical Growth**: Balance reflection with real-life strategies.
5. **Respect for Relational Systems**: See each person within larger systems—families, communities—as context for positive change.

### Brand Personality

- **Human** – Warm, approachable, genuine
- **Empathic** – Compassionate, understanding
- **Personal** – Conversational, authentic, jargon-free
- **Collaborative** – Partner language ("together," "let's explore")
- **Inclusive** – Sensitive to cultural backgrounds; respectful

### Tone of Voice

- **Friendly & Encouraging** – Supportive, uplifting
- **Clear & Practical** – Reflections paired with actionable steps
- **Respectful & Non-judgmental** – Guidance without blame
- **Concise & Accessible** – Short paragraphs, clear headings

---

## 2. Logo Usage

Primary logo: Calm, approachable Tree of Life reflecting growth and connection.

- **Clear Space**: Maintain generous space for legibility.
- **Variations**: Single-color (Charcoal Gray) for print; monochrome for dark backgrounds.
- **Minimum Size**: Keep large enough for all details to remain clear.

---

## 3. Color Palette

Balances warmth with calm professionalism.

1. **Brand Blueish** – `#359BB7`: Large backgrounds, primary brand color
2. **Accent Greenish** – `#61A188`: Supporting backgrounds, subtle highlights
3. **Terracotta** – `#D6A756`: Call-to-action buttons, interactive elements

**Usage**: Maintain WCAG 2.1 AA contrast. Pair Charcoal Gray text with lighter backgrounds. Use Terracotta sparingly for key CTAs.

---

## 4. Typography

**Typeface**: Nunito (Weights: 400, 600, 700)

- **Headlines (H1–H3)**: Bold (700), prominent sizes
- **Body Text**: Regular (400), comfortable line spacing
- **Subheads/Emphasis**: Semi-Bold (600)

**Responsive**: Scale headline sizes on smaller screens; maintain comfortable line heights across devices.

---

## 5. Iconography & Imagery

**Icons**
- Style: Rounded, minimal, growth-oriented
- Use: Smaller for inline actions; larger for hero sections
- Colors: Align with Brand Blueish or Accent Greenish

**Photography**
- Mood: Warm, natural images evoking empathy and calm
- Content: Diverse individuals, realistic settings, nature-inspired visuals
- Emphasis: Authentic moments showing people in conversation, personal reflection

---

## 6. Motion & Interaction

**General**: Subtle, purposeful animations—supportive, not distracting.

- **Transitions**: Under 300ms, gentle easing; fade or crossfade pages
- **Hover & Focus**: Subtle color transitions or faint shadow; minimal scaling

---

## 7. Grid & Layout

- **Grid**: Flexible 12-column structure, adapts to smaller devices
- **Spacing**: Consistent units (multiples of 4 or 8) for calm, uncluttered look
- **Responsive**: Mobile-first; ample whitespace for serene, welcoming feel

## 8. Tone of Voice & Copy

**Style**
- **First Person**: Speak from my perspective ("I," "my," "let's explore").
- **Second Person**: Address clients as "you."
- **Personality**: Empathetic, supportive, approachable; calm, nurturing voice.

**Example Phrasing**
- "I'm here to help you discover new perspectives."
- "Let's work together to find meaningful change."

**Best Practices**
- **Warmth & Encouragement**: "I understand," "Let's explore how we can…"
- **Clarity Over Jargon**: Avoid clinical terms; break down complex ideas.
- **Concise Paragraphs**: Short blocks, bullet points for easy reading.
- **Active Voice**: "I help you" not "You are helped by me."

---

## 9. Accessibility

- **Color & Contrast**: High text contrast against backgrounds.
- **Reduced Motion**: Respect user settings to disable animations.
- **Clear Labels**: Screen-reader-accessible forms and error messages.

## 10. Future Adaptability

- **Theme Variations**: Potential dark mode or high-contrast theme while retaining core colors.
- **Video & Print**: Consistent visuals and voice for print, presentations, video.
- **Ongoing**: Regular updates as brand grows.

---

Mikrouli Systemic Therapy's presence remains **calm, supportive, and professional**—reflecting the empathetic, approachable, warm experience clients deserve.
