# Brand

This is the summary source for identity and positioning. For the detailed writing system, see `docs/copy.md`; for the generated tone model, see `docs/voice-profile.md`.

## Purpose

Mikrouli Systemic Therapy offers calm, supportive therapy for individuals, couples, and families navigating stress, transitions, and relational patterns.

## Core pillars

- Human and personal
- Empathic and collaborative
- Inclusive and culturally aware
- Practical and growth-oriented

## Positioning

- Support clients with warmth and clarity.
- Help people understand patterns without blame.
- Balance reflection with actionable steps.
- Create a safe space for change.

## Visual identity

- Palette: blue, green, warm terracotta, and neutral charcoal
- Typography: Nunito with clear, readable hierarchy
- Imagery: warm, natural, authentic, and human
- Motion: subtle and supportive, never noisy
